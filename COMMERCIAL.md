# Commercial and OEM licensing

Kiro is free software under the [GPLv3](LICENSE). That is the version everyone
gets, and it is not going away.

A separate commercial licence exists for the cases the GPLv3 cannot serve:

- **Bundling Kiro in a closed-source product.** If your product links Kiro's code
  into something you ship without source, the GPLv3 does not permit it. A
  commercial licence does.
- **OEM and white-label builds.** Your own name, logo and accent colour, a fixed
  layout, a preconfigured `settings.json`, silent installation, the auto-updater
  off, and support for the Windows build you standardise on.
- **Kiosks, digital signage, gaming cafés and showrooms.** A locked-down Kiro that
  guests cannot reconfigure.
- **Managed deployment.** An MSI or Intune/GPO package, a central configuration,
  and a version you control the update timing of.

## Pricing

Quoted per engagement, based on seats and the amount of customisation:

- **Commercial licence** — from €500 per year, per organisation.
- **White-label / OEM build** — from €800 one-off, plus a yearly maintenance option.

Students, non-profits and other open-source projects do not need a commercial
licence. Use the GPLv3 version.
