The **Kiro** name, the Kiro logo, the mascot and the wordmark are trademarks of
Furkan Taha Akkose. The source code is licensed under the GPLv3 (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)); this document covers the name and the branding only.

## What this does not change

This policy grants no rights over the code and takes none away. Everything the
GPLv3 allows you to do with the code, you can still do — including redistributing
it, with or without a fee. Trademark law and copyright law are separate, and this
file only speaks about the name.

## What you may do

- Fork the repository, read it, change it, and publish your changes.
- Say that your project is **based on Kiro**, or **a fork of Kiro**, as long as
  the wording is factual and does not imply it is the official build.
- Use the name in reviews, articles, videos, comparisons, tutorials, package
  descriptions and academic work.
- Keep the name in the source files you did not modify, and in the
  `package.json` description of an unmodified copy.
- Link to <https://github.com/fruyorsan/Kiro>.

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified build under the **Kiro** name, or under a name confusingly
  similar to it, without written permission.
- Use the Kiro logo, the mascot or the wordmark as the icon, splash screen,
  installer artwork or store listing of a modified build.
- Claim or imply that your build is official, endorsed, sponsored or supported by
  the Kiro project.
- Register the name, the logo or a confusingly similar domain, GitHub
  organisation, package name or social handle.
- Use the name in a way that suggests a commercial relationship that does not
  exist.

If you fork Kiro, give your build its own name and its own icon. That is the whole
requirement, and it is easy to meet.

## Commercial use

Selling a copy of Kiro, or of a fork, is a question for the GPLv3, not for this
policy — the license permits it and this file neither adds to nor subtracts from
that. What this policy rules out is selling something **as Kiro**. A paid build
that carries the Kiro name, logo or mascot needs written permission first.

If you want to ship Kiro inside a commercial product, or want the official
branding on a commercial build, get in touch rather than guess:

- <https://github.com/fruyorsan/Kiro/issues>
- <https://buymeacoffee.com/fruyorsan>

## Reporting misuse

If you find a build, a store listing or a domain that uses the Kiro name or logo
and is not official, open an issue. For a GitHub repository or account, use
GitHub's trademark complaint form instead.
